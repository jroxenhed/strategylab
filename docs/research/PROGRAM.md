## Research Axioms

Hard-won findings that steer premise design. Authoritative until experimentally overturned — and the first one HAS been partially overturned; its honest status is part of the record.

- **Price leads, filings trail — STATUS: WORKING HYPOTHESIS (downgraded by John, 2026-06-06).** Information diffusion order: insiders → price → filings → analysts → screeners. A quarterly filing is the *slowest public record* of things informed money acted on months earlier, so any screen using filings as the **trigger** structurally trades against people who already moved. GPRO-2015 and ENPH-2023 weren't anomalies; they were the rule (run-1/run-2 miss-list autopsy, confirmed by John's lived trading experience). **Honest evidential status:** (a) the case-study base is crashed names only — a subset generalized to the whole market untested; (b) the one formal test (epistemics ablation, U8) confirmed the filings-null half but FAILED to confirm the directional half (price did not beat filings; Δ=−3.00pp, CI [−11.45,+8.18]); (c) per F340 that test ran on a ~3pp-MDE instrument, so even the null half is "nothing visible to a blind instrument"; (d) the Form 4 corollary is untested (R-1). **Heterogeneity is the live question:** information speed should scale with attention — filings may genuinely LEAD price for low-coverage small names (where classic earnings drift lived). Do not let this axiom pre-judge designs; it is itself on the test bench (F347). Corollaries (same downgraded status): filings still plausible as **vetoes/filters**; **Form 4 plausibly upstream of price** (untested → R-1); the ablation that half-tested this is U8.

- **The test design is itself a premise — charter the clock, not just the thresholds.** The 2026-06-05 program escaped its idea-level premise (pond → premise-free) but unknowingly carried the pond-era *apparatus* into every experiment: quarterly decision dates, ~50-pick cohorts, same-day-median scoring. F340 measured that apparatus: minimum detectable effect ~3.0pp at 80% power, so any realistic-sized edge (1–2pp) was guaranteed to grade "not provable" *regardless of the premise* — four different hypotheses hit the same ceiling, and the ceiling was the one thing they shared. Standing rules: (1) every charter states its sampling clock and its design's MDE (`backend/research/power_audit.py` before locking); (2) "no edge found" is only meaningful down to the design's MDE — below that it's "couldn't have seen it," a different finding. (Diagnosis John's: "it got stuck in the initial premise" — the premise was the instrument.)

- **Instrument-first budgeting (John, 2026-06-06: "testing design is as important as the premise — if not more").** A bad premise in a good instrument costs one clean kill. A good premise in a bad instrument gets a true edge labeled "nothing" and abandoned — the worst outcome available. All seven bugs caught on 2026-06-05 were measurement bugs; zero were idea bugs; ideas are cheap (48/hour in ideation). Spend generously on instrument validation (it compounds), let premises be plentiful, and gate hard only at confirm touches.

## Standing methodology rules

1. **Pre-registered charters** (hash-fingerprinted, authored by an outcome-blind agent), **sealed confirm grading** by a fresh agent, **alpha/FDR accounting** — confirm touches are the scarce resource; explore looks are cheap and FDR-ledgered.
2. **F338 real-data smoke probes** with pre-stated face-validity anchors before any new instrument's output is believed (synthetic suites share their author's blind spots — proven 3× on 2026-06-05 and 7 more times in the 2026-06-06 reviews).
   **Corollary (2026-06-06, the 108-entry ledger pollution):** analysis code must NEVER default an FDR-ledger write to the real ledger — `ledger_path=None` means SKIP with a warning, and real runs pass the real path explicitly. The first R-1 test suite defaulted there and wrote 108 synthetic fixture entries into `fdr_ledger.json` (caught and deleted before any real entry existed). Writing the permanent alpha-accounting record must be a deliberate act.
3. **Charter the clock + state the MDE** (axiom 2). 4. **Era-consistency**: explore survivors must hold sign across explore sub-eras; confirm verdicts report per-sub-era breakdown within the single touch (John's walk-forward instinct, 2026-06-06).
5. **Plain-English checkpoint at every milestone** — verdicts, gates, program closes (CLAUDE.local.md rule; jargon must be defined inline, always).
6a. **The standard lens stack (assembled from John's critiques, 2026-06-06).** Every verdict reports ONE primary bar (universe excess — the tradeable edge) plus four honesty lenses, none of which add pass/fail bars but any of which can cap or qualify the verdict: **peer excess** (same-SIC siblings — did it beat its herd or ride it? fail → CONFIRMED-SECTOR-CARRIED, F349), **era breakdown** (explore sub-era sign agreement gates confirm; confirm reports per-era), **regime breakdown** (effect per market-weather state from regime_states.json; calm/neutral/stormy reported, crisis never load-bearing at 6 days/decade; single-regime effects flagged regime-carried, and regime-dependent playbooks scale Desk conviction by current weather), and the **perturbation band** (sign stability under constant wiggles). Lenses report; the primary bar stays singular — multiplying bars multiplies false discoveries.

6b. **Dose-response over cliff-edges (John, 2026-06-06: "what if they bought within 6 days instead of 5? what if the sum is $49k?").** Binary thresholds discard information and manufacture fragile findings. Charters prefer: ONE frozen continuous score (the formula is hashed like a threshold — fuzziness licenses zero tuning), a monotonicity headline hypothesis (top-vs-bottom quintile + trend across quintiles; a real effect should scale with its dose), and a pre-registered perturbation band (sign stability under small parameter wiggles; a sign flip caps the verdict at WEAKENED — if the finding dies when the window moves a day, the finding *was* the window). Binary gates survive only at the execution layer (The Desk needs a buy/don't line; even there, score can drive position size). Size-relative normalization (e.g. insider dollars ÷ point-in-time market cap from cached shares × price) is the default for any dollar-denominated signal.
