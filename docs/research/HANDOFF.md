## Where we are, in one paragraph

**The PEAD/earnings-surprise premise the census pointed at was built and tested — and it's a clean NULL.** We shipped F348 (the fundamental-surprise payload layer: 8 point-in-time deltas vs a company's own filing history, estimate-free), then used it to run F370 explore-0: does that surprise predict drift *after* the 10-Q/10-K filing? It does not — all three dose scores (earnings_yoy, revenue_yoy, composite) come in below their detection floor and statistically insignificant at the 63-day horizon; earnings-surprise even points the wrong way. John's "measure the gap" lens (link each filing to its preceding 8-K item-2.02 earnings announcement) matched 67% of events and showed a +1.41% mean announce→filing drift that **does not scale with the surprise** (corr ≈ 0.04) — so the estimate-free own-history surprise carries little return information in *either* window, reinforcing F371 (the positive raw means are the size/survivorship baseline). **No confirm alpha spent, no standardized-SUE built** — the explore-as-power-gate did its job: the census predicted underpowering, explore confirmed near-zero point estimates, and we stopped cheaply (no repeat of R-1b's wasted confirm). The F348 layer + the explore harness + the gap lens are validated, reusable instruments for the next premise. **The next research direction is open — John's call (the census family's most-natural dose just got killed).**
