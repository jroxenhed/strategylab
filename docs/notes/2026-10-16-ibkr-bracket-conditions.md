# IBKR bracket stops: conditions to turn on IBKR_BRACKET_STOPS (F452)

F449 (2026-10-16) built an IBKR bracket entry (market parent with transmit=False, child stop with parentId) and held it back behind the env flag IBKR_BRACKET_STOPS, off by default. With the flag off, an IBKR entry is one SMART market order and the stop is polled, like Alpaca shorts. A two-reviewer wave confirmed IB-01 (an orphan child stop after a clientId rotation) and IB-02 (a stale position qty after a child fill). Turn the flag on only after a paper Gateway test shows every condition below.


Each item needs a code fix and a test on a paper Gateway (port 4002, Read-Only API unchecked) that shows the stated result.

1. **IB-01 (client id).** Stop rotating the client id on reconnect (or pin `IBKR_CLIENT_ID` to Gateway's Master API client id). Close and close_all must call `reqAllOpenOrdersAsync()` before looking for child stops, and refuse to send the market close (raise, so the bot counts and alerts) while any closing-side order on the symbol is from another client id or not yet cancelled. Paper test: place a bracket, force a Gateway reconnect, then close from the bot. The child must be found and cancelled before the close, the account must end flat with no open orders, and no second fill.
2. **IB-02 (stale qty after a child fill).** After the cancel wait, trust the trades, not `ib.positions()`: subtract the filled qty of the waited trades, or refuse the close when any of them filled. Paper test: put the child stop at the market so it fills during the bot's close. Only one exit fill, and the account ends flat, not reversed.
3. **IB-03 (fill price and time).** Fill `filled_avg_price` and `filled_at` from `trade.fills` when `avgFillPrice` is 0. Sort `get_orders` by fill time, newest first, and map the type names to Alpaca's (stop, market, ...). Without `filled_at`, LM-3 never accepts an IBKR fill as proof. Paper test: let a child stop fill while the backend is stopped, restart, and check the bot books one stop_loss row at the real fill price on its first tick.
4. **IB-04 (cancel not confirmed).** If a closing-side order is still open after the cancel wait, raise instead of sending the close; the bot retries next tick. Paper test: delay the cancel (for example, close during a Gateway reconnect). No market close goes out while the child is live, and the next tick closes cleanly.
5. **IB-05 (child rejection visible).** Return the child order id, and have the bot log and alert (not pause) when the child is rejected. Paper test: send a stop price with a bad tick size. The bot logs and alerts that the protective stop was rejected, and the polling stop still covers the position.
6. **IB-06 (code 10147).** Fix the comment (10147 is "order to cancel not found") and treat it as transient for cancels. Paper test: cancel an order that has just filled. The bot does not pause.
7. **Whole flow on paper, long and short.** A bracket entry shows both orders in Gateway, with the child live only after the parent fills. A normal bot exit cancels the child and closes once. A child stop fill is booked once as stop_loss at its fill price. `/sell` and `close_all` leave no orphan stop. A DAY child is gone at the session end, and the polling stop covers the position overnight (or John chooses GTC).
